# Stick HQ

*From jig to doorstep.*

Stick HQ keeps every online order you place in one place on your own PC: it reads the order, shipping and
delivery emails your stores send, and shows what you bought, what's on the way, what got canceled and where
everything is going.

## Get it

Stick HQ is for members of its Discord server. Join here: https://discord.gg/7UGtP7vYp2

1. Download **StickHQ-Setup** from the [latest release](https://github.com/Stick-HQ/stick-hq/releases/latest).
2. Run it. Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"* the first time: choose **More info > Run anyway**.
   (The app isn't signed with a paid code-signing certificate yet.)
3. Open Stick HQ and sign in with Discord.

Updates install from inside the app (**Settings > Updates**). Every update is signed, and the app refuses one
that isn't.

## Privacy

Everything you add stays on your PC. Passwords and keys are encrypted with your Windows login. The app talks
only to your own mailboxes, the stores' image servers, Discord (to check your access) and GitHub (for updates).

## Requirements

Windows 10 or 11, and Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.
